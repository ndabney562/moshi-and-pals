Privacy Policy

Moshi and Pals · Effective October 30, 2026

The short version: Moshi and Pals does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, in the iPhone app or in the browser.

What stays on your device

Your saves stay on your own iPhone or in your own browser and are never sent to us. They hold your settings, records, unlocked Pals, achievements and a paused shift. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, erases them.

Game Center (iPhone app)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your best score for each difficulty and the achievements you unlock to Apple's Game Center, so they appear on its leaderboards and in your profile. Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy, and your Game Center settings control who sees your nickname and scores. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

The browser version

The browser game is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads the page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game only asks its own site whether a newer version exists. The online leaderboard is turned off; if it is ever turned on, this page will be updated first.

Children

Moshi and Pals does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/ndabney562/moshi-and-pals/issues.
