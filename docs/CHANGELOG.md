# Changelog

## v0.30.0: Selina and Elaine at the front desk, and staff upgrades
- **Two new helpers: Selina and Elaine, the front desk PSRs.** Hire them on the Residency Board (15 CME each, under the new **STAFF** tab) and their pills join the power-up drops.
- **Grab one and she opens the front desk.** Every patient lines up in front of RECEPTION and complains about the wait ("I've been here since NINE.") instead of committing health code violations, and she checks them in and sends them home one at a time. Each check-in counts as cleaning up the patient, with the usual reward.
- **Selina** checks people in fast (every 2.5 s, +$5). **Elaine** is slower (every 4 s) but collects the copay at check-in (+$25).
- **Staff upgrades.** The Equipment Locker has a new **STAFF** shelf: Omar, Alan, Donald, Selina and Elaine each get three upgrades you keep for good, paid with banked Copays once they're on staff: **Coffee Run** ($500, +20% damage; the PSRs bring in 20% more Copays per check-in), **Overtime** ($1,200, they stay 50% longer) and **Employee of the Month** ($2,500, +15% damage and they work 20% faster). Overtime is only for the front desk: Omar, Alan and Donald already stay all shift once they're out, so they skip it.
- **Donald waits in X-ray now, like the scribes in their exam rooms.** Open the X-ray room and he comes out and helps for the rest of the shift (the first time also unlocks him for good). No more Donald pill, and walking into X-ray no longer calls him for 20 s.
- **You can see who's waiting.** A dark silhouette of Omar, Alan or Donald stands in the locked room they're waiting in, so you know which door to open.
- **Once they're out, they're there for good.** Omar, Alan and Donald can't be hurt and stay on shift until it ends.

## v0.29.0: Germ vents and Prior Auth barricades
- **Germs come in through vents** now: green, bubbling floor grates at random spots in every open room. At the start of every round the vents move somewhere new, so the same corner is never safe twice.
- **BLOCK a vent for $50.** Stand by one and press USE: a PRIOR AUTH barricade goes up and germs have to wait for approval. They come in through another vent instead, but every germ that tries this one pushes on it (BANG!), and after 8 pushes or 30 seconds it gives way. A bar under it shows how much it has left. If every vent is blocked, germs pile up behind the barricades until one breaks.
- Bosses don't wait for prior authorization.
- **The scribes and Donald block vents too.** While they're helping, each one tapes off a vent near them every 15 s with your Copays, as long as you'd still have $100 left.

## v0.28.5: The gel burns
- **Gel Launcher gel now burns.** Germs standing in it take 12 damage a second (18 with Family Size), and keep burning for 1.5 s after they crawl out. Bosses aren't slowed by gel, but they do burn. Burning germs flicker orange.
- Overlapping gel doesn't stack the burn; the hottest puddle counts. Tool effectiveness and damage bonuses (Pals, perks) apply; STAT! crits don't.

## v0.28.4: Every tool looks different

- SWAP changes the tool in your hand too, and the spray comes out of the new tool's nozzle.

## v0.27.1: Germs stop getting stuck
- **Germs no longer jam together.** A crowd of germs used to brake each other to a crawl wherever they touched, so packs looked glued in place. They now shove each other aside and keep coming.

- Germs keep to the middle of halls and doorways instead of scraping along walls, so big ones like the Chonk Coccus don't wedge on corners.

  - **Spiral Spit:** one of them spins and spits a ring of globs.

## v0.19.0: Milestone 17, Today's Shift
- **Today's Shift** (📋 on the title screen): a daily challenge. Everyone gets the same shift on the same day: the date picks the random rolls and two modifiers, shown before you CLOCK IN. Always on Urgent. The title screen and the Discharge Summary show today's best, and your first finished Today's Shift each day earns +2 CME.
- **Six modifiers:**
  - **Flu Season:** Viral Sprinters come in twice as often, from round 1.
  - **Power Outage:** the clinic is dark except your flashlight's circle around you. The HUD stays lit.
  - **Inspection Day:** Spotless rounds pay triple.
  - **Short-Staffed:** nobody restocks the carts, so restocks cost triple ($120).
  - **Construction:** each round from round 2, one room you opened closes for "renovations" until the round ends. Never the room you're in, and never one you'd need to get back to the waiting room. Germs fit under the door.
  - **Full Moon:** germs come in odd sizes (their hitboxes don't change), the floor is moonlit, and patients walk backwards.
- **Difficulty** (🩺 on the title screen, tap to switch): **Walk-In** (softer germs, gentler bumps, better Copays), **Urgent** (the game as before) and **Trauma Center** (tougher germs that hit harder and keep coming). Records are kept per difficulty, so a Walk-In shift never beats your Urgent record. Your old records count as Urgent. The Discharge Summary names the difficulty.
- 3 new achievements: *Clocked In* (finish a Today's Shift), *Level One Trauma* (round 10 on Trauma Center), *Working in the Dark* (round 8 in a Power Outage). The achievement list goes to three columns on phones.
- For testers: `?mods=powerOutage,fullMoon` forces modifiers, `?seed=123` fixes the run's random rolls.
